## Determinism facts confirmed today

- Rapier deterministic-compat 0.21.0 in Node: two fresh worlds give identical state after 480 steps; `World.restoreSnapshot` then 600 steps equals the uninterrupted run; snapshot about 2.1 KB per body at 2 bodies (4,295 bytes). Body handles are doubles with meaningful bit patterns.
- V8 on Apple Silicon: Math.sin, cos, tan, exp, log, atan, asin, acos, sinh, cosh, tanh, atan2, pow and hypot differ from a pure-JavaScript fdlibm port by 1 to 2 ulp on 0.01% to 15% of inputs (fused multiply-add in V8's compiled C++), while log2 and cbrt match exactly. This is the engine-to-engine variation dmath removes.
- Dawn (webgpu 0.6.2) runs WGSL compute in Node on the Mac; its native module crashes inside Vitest's forked worker but works in plain Node (ADR 0003).
