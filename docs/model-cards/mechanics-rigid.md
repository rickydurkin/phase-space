# Model card: Rigid bodies (mechanics)
Reality badge: Real
Equations: Newton-Euler rigid-body dynamics with contact, Coulomb friction and restitution, solved by Rapier 0.21.0 (cross-platform deterministic build) using an impulse-based solver with 4 solver iterations and 1 internal PGS iteration per substep (Rapier defaults, read back from IntegrationParameters).
Assumptions and valid range: bodies are perfectly rigid; shapes are cuboids and balls in P00; gameplay scenes stay within about 2 km of the origin because Rapier works in single precision (MASTER 5.8). Friction and restitution combine by Rapier's default rule (average).
Numerical method and timestep: fixed tick 1/120 s with two substeps of 1/240 s; symplectic Euler integration inside Rapier; continuous collision detection off in P00 (enabled per body for fast projectiles in P1). Gravity 9.80665 m/s^2 (CONSTANTS.g0).
Couplings in and out: in P00 none. From P1: friction and impact heating to thermal, buoyancy and drag from fluids, forces from fields. Energy stores reported to the ledger every tick: kinetic (translational plus rotational from principal inertia) and gravitational (relative to the y = 0 plane).
Data sources: material densities per scene definition; wood 700 kg/m^3 for the P00 cube (Engineering ToolBox, softwood range 350 to 900, est.).
Validation: M1 free fall, position at 2 s within 0.5% (measured drop 19.62324 m against 19.61330 m, 0.051% high, with symplectic Euler at 240 Hz on 15/10/2026). Golden replay cube-drop, 1200 ticks, hash 3374e5261804bf27 at physics 0.1.0.
Known limits and what is not modelled: no rolling resistance, no joints yet, no aerodynamics, no fracture; contact losses are not yet booked as transfers, so the ledger reports them as unbooked loss (allowed) rather than friction heat (P1).
Player-facing summary (en-GB): Solid objects that do not bend. Gravity, contact, friction and bounce are solved by the Rapier engine in fixed steps of 1/240 s, so the same start always gives the same motion, on every computer.
